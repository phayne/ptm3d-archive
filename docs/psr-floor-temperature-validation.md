# Haworth permanently shadowed floor: history of the comparison

This page records how the Haworth comparison reached its current state. The current numbers
are in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md) (Figs. 8 and 9).

In the earlier comparison (2026, before the revision), the model's summer-maximum temperature
at the central permanently shadowed floor of Haworth (87.45°S, 2.1°W) was about 45 K against
about 65 K from Diviner (Williams et al. 2019), a cold bias of about 20 K. An H-matrix defect
that zeroed floor-to-wall coupling above about 100,000 faces had been found and fixed before
that comparison.

The revision rebuilt the view factors with a Monte Carlo estimator on the correct surface side
and added sub-facet roughness (beaming), which is appropriate on this 240 m site mesh.

In the current result, at the Haworth SE-rim site, over 2,970 cold-trap cells, the median
summer-maximum bias is −0.80 K (IQR 11.19 K) with beaming and −10.30 K (IQR 10.38 K)
without it. The central-floor comparison described above has not been repeated with the
corrected view factors.
